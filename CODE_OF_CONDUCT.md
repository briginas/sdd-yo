# Code of conduct

## Our standard

SDD Yo welcomes respectful, professional, and inclusive collaboration. Focus
discussion on the work, give actionable feedback, assume good faith while
still examining evidence, and respect other participants' privacy and stated
boundaries.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory or degrading
language, threats, doxxing, sustained disruption, sexualized attention, and
publishing private communications without permission.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review
channels, and public representation of the project community.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through a private
contact method listed on their GitHub profile. If no private method is
available, open a minimal issue requesting private contact without disclosing
the incident publicly. Security vulnerabilities follow
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) instead.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, reject contributions, issue a
warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently exclude a
participant. Reports will be handled as privately as practical, with attention
to the safety of the people involved.
