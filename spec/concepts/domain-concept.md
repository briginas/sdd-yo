# Domain Concept

## Definition <!-- sdd:definition -->

A Domain Concept defines a significant object, role, event, state vocabulary,
or term used across Requirements.

## Identity <!-- sdd:identity -->

A Domain Concept has one stable random `CON-XXXXXXXX` identifier independent of
its title and document path.

## Relations <!-- sdd:relations -->

- relates-to: [CON-9F69CC0E — Requirement](requirement.md)
- relates-to: [CON-FC16381E — Fingerprint](fingerprint.md)

## Rationale <!-- sdd:rationale -->

Domain Concepts centralize reusable vocabulary and make indirect semantic
impact discoverable. Verifiable rules about a concept remain separate
Requirements rather than hidden prose.
