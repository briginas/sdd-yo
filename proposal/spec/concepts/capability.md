# Capability

## Definition <!-- sdd:definition -->

A Capability is a coherent, user-meaningful area of implemented product
behavior composed of one or more Requirements.

## Identity <!-- sdd:identity -->

A Capability has one stable random `CAP-XXXXXXXX` identifier. Its identity is
independent of its title, document path, and internal document partitioning.

## Relations <!-- sdd:relations -->

- relates-to: [CON-9F69CC0E — Requirement](requirement.md)
- relates-to: [CON-77D857DB — Document](document.md)

## Rationale <!-- sdd:rationale -->

Capabilities give humans a useful reading and QA scope while Requirements
remain the atomic normative units.
